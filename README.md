# FitnessCoach
Dashboard for Macros and workout plan
A clean, dark-mode fitness dashboard that calculates personalised calorie goals, macros, water intake, daily steps, and generates a weekly workout schedule based on your stats and preferences.

## Features

- **Personalised nutrition targets** — calories, protein, carbs, and fat calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR formula
- **Water & step goals** — adjusted to your fitness goal and body weight
- **BMI calculator** — computed from your height and weight
- **Flexible unit support** — switch between cm/inches and kg/lbs at any time
- **Four workout splits** — 3-day full body, 4-day upper/lower, 5-day push/pull/legs, 6-day bro split
- **Weekly schedule view** — today's session is highlighted automatically
- **Three fitness goals** — weight loss, muscle gain, or maintenance (each adjusts calorie targets)
- **No dependencies** — pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no installs, no tracking


## How to Use

1. Open the **Profile** tab
2. Set your preferred units (cm/in, kg/lbs)
3. Enter your age, gender, height, current weight, and goal weight
4. Choose your activity level, fitness goal, and preferred workout split
5. Hit **Calculate my goals**
6. View your personalised targets on the **Dashboard** tab
7. See your weekly exercise plan on the **Schedule** tab
